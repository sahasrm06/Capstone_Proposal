# Original data access

Pfab, K. (2024). Publication data: Data of automated optical inspection of surface-mounted technology electronic production (Version 2). Mendeley Data. https://doi.org/10.17632/99jzmh9658.2

Landing page: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/99jzmh9658/2

Download dataset.csv and mapping.json from the original provider, or use scripts/download_data.py. This repository does not redistribute raw data.

The source metadata lists CC BY NC 3.0 alongside a Siemens legal notice permitting only non-commercial testing in connection with the associated Data in Brief paper. This capstone is an academic evaluation of that published resource. Preserve the source notice; do not assume unrestricted redistribution or commercial use. The complete original notice remains on the source landing page.

Associated paper: Pfab, K., Eichler, R., Mallandur, A., & Rothering, M. (2024). Data of automated optical inspection of surface-mounted technology electronic production. Data in Brief, 53, 110110. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2024.110110
